One World, One Dream

About Sibaihua University

An Independent Institution Dedicated to Global Inquiries & Discovery

Sibaihua University, Chiang Mai Campus is a comprehensive, self-governing university rooted in the scenic highlands of Doi Inthanon. Inspired by the universal vision of "One World, One Dream," the university unites classical humanism, Asian civilizational exchanges, and cutting-edge science.

Our campus ethos is defined by an enduring commitment to intellectual liberty, universal equality, and selfless altruistic devotion. We uphold an open, empathetic sanctuary that champions scientific and humanitarian progress, embracing every identity with unconditional dignity, universal respect, and full allyship for the LGBTQ+ community.

With administrative and supervisory arms stationed at King Power Mahanakhon in Bangkok and direct liaison hubs in Beijing, Sibaihua University bridges Southeast Asian intellectual vitality with worldwide academic networks.

Fundamental Science & Natural Laws

Faculty of Science

Dedicated to rigorous foundational research across Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, and Biology, alongside deep empirical inquiry into natural laws and ecological systems. Anchored at the Doi Inthanon alpine research stations and marine observatories, the faculty bridges pure theoretical sciences with applied environmental discovery.
