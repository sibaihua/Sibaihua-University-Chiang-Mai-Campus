About Sibaihua University

An Independent Institution Dedicated to Global Inquiry & Discovery

Sibaihua University, Chiang Mai Campus is a comprehensive, self-governing university rooted in the scenic highlands of Doi Inthanon. Inspired by the universal vision of "One World, One Dream," the university brings together classical humanism, Asian civilizational exchange, and cutting-edge science.

Our campus ethos is defined by an enduring commitment to intellectual liberty, universal equality, and selfless altruism. We uphold an open and empathetic sanctuary that champions scientific and humanitarian progress, embracing every identity with unconditional dignity, universal respect, and full allyship with the LGBTQ+ community.

With administrative and supervisory offices at King Power Mahanakhon in Bangkok and a direct liaison hub in Beijing, Sibaihua University connects Southeast Asian intellectual vitality with academic networks around the world.

Institutional Framework

University Charter

The constitutive instruments and standing principles of Sibaihua University.

University Council

The Council determines institutional statutes, approves long-term strategy, and oversees the Finance and Supervisory Committees. Its seat is the Bangkok Executive Center.

President's Office

The chief executive organ of the University. It executes Council resolutions and directs academic affairs, scientific research, and the operations of every faculty and campus.

Faculties

The University comprises the Faculty of Science, the Faculty of Arts, the Faculty of Informatics, the School of Economics, the Faculty of Fine Arts, and the Faculty of Social Sciences, all anchored at the Chiang Mai Campus.

Academic Freedom

The University affirms the freedom of teaching, inquiry, and publication. Scholarship is conducted without ideological coercion, and diverse traditions of thought are welcomed as equals.

Student Rights

Every member is entitled to dignity, equitable access to learning, and protection from discrimination. The University upholds full allyship with the LGBTQ+ community and treats all identities with unconditional respect.

Institutional Principles

The University is governed by intellectual liberty, universal equality, and selfless altruism. It exists as an open sanctuary for scientific and humanitarian progress under the motto “One World, One Dream.”
